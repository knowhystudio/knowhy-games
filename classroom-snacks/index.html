HOW TO PLAY

One snack at a time.

01

Pick a snack

Each snack takes a different time to eat and gives a different amount of fullness and points.

02

Wait for an opening

Watch where the teacher stands and faces. You can stop eating halfway through.

03

Hide it in time

In timed mode, reach the fullness goal and last until the bell rings.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY

Finish the class or chase a record.

◷

Timed challenge

Fill up before class ends and make it through the round.

∞

Endless challenge

No class timer and no snack limit. How high can you score?

Both modes have four difficulties. Personal records are saved separately.
