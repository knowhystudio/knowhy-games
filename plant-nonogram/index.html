HOW TO PLAY

Read the clues, fill the grid.

01

Follow the numbers

Each clue gives the lengths of consecutive filled runs in that row or column.

02

Mark what is empty

Tap to fill, long-press for X marks. Finished lines cross themselves out.

03

Reveal the picture

Clear the grid to unlock the finished pixel plant and a real plant fun fact.
